What this POC does — and doesn’t

  • Audio only, one host and one guest. No accounts, channels, recordings or history.
  • Pairing data stays in these browser tabs and is never sent to an app-owned signalling service.
  • A direct connection can fail behind restrictive NATs or firewalls. Retry in another network if it does.
