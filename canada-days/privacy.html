Offday

Privacy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Offday is a static calendar reference. It does not have an application server or user accounts.

Google Analytics

Offday uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page views and general site interactions, such as scrolling and outbound link clicks. Google may receive technical information about your browser and device and use cookies for measurement. Advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled. Analytics does not receive your selected work region or precise location.

Location and preferences

If you ask the app to suggest a work region, your browser provides a location estimate for that suggestion. The app compares it with local region bounds; coordinates are not saved or sent to Google Analytics. Your chosen language, calendar and region are saved only in your browser.

Your choices

You can control or clear cookies in your browser and opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Offday does not use analytics on localhost.
