Capsule Shelf

Privacy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Capsule Shelf is a static personal coffee inventory app. This notice explains what information the app stores, how optional Google Drive sync works, and how Google Analytics is used.

Information stored on your device

Your capsule quantities, stock thresholds, favourites, notes, and language preference are stored in your browser on your device. If you connect Google Drive, the app also keeps your Google account identifier and email address in browser storage so it can remember which account you connected. Your OAuth access token is kept in memory while the page is open and is not saved in browser storage.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Capsule Shelf uses Google sign-in to identify your account and the Google Drive drive.appdata permission to read and update one settings JSON file in the app-specific hidden data folder. The app does not request access to your other Drive files. Your settings and access token are sent by your browser directly to Google APIs; Capsule Shelf has no application server and does not receive or store them.

You can disconnect Google Drive from the sync control in the app. This removes the remembered account from this browser and stops future syncs. It does not delete the settings file in your Google Drive; you can remove Capsule Shelf's data or revoke its access in your Google Account settings.

Sharing and service providers

Capsule Shelf uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page views and general site interactions, such as scrolling and outbound link clicks. Google may receive technical information about your browser and device, use cookies for measurement, and infer a general location from your network address. Advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled. Analytics does not read or receive your capsule quantities, favourites, notes, or other settings stored in the app.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Google processes Analytics, sign-in, and Drive requests under its own privacy terms. You can control or clear cookies in your browser and opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

If you use the feedback link, it opens GitHub and anything you submit there may be public. Do not include information you want to keep private.

Retention and choices

Browser data remains on your device until you clear it. Google Drive settings remain in your account until you delete them. You can use the app without connecting Google Drive. Analytics is enabled on the published site and does not run on localhost.
